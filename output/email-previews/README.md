# Contact email previews

Open `notification.html` or `confirmation.html` locally to review the matching navy/coral templates. PNG screenshots are also included.

These previews use fictional enquiry details and example.test addresses/links; no email was sent. The real templates use the configured portfolio URL, the visitor's validated email, and the configured owner inbox. Portraits are embedded in these previews with a data URL; production email embeds the JPEG as a Content-ID MIME attachment.

The templates were checked in Chromium at desktop/mobile widths and composed with Nodemailer's local stream transport. Actual Gmail/Outlook inbox rendering and delivery remain unverified.
